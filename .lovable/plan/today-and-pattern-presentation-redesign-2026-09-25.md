# Today and Pattern presentation redesign

## Goal
Make personalized meaning the first thing people encounter, with calculation details available afterward rather than competing for attention.

## Changes
- Reorder Today so the headline and reading lead on mobile and desktop.
- Condense the opening timing facts to phase, strength, and exact timing; move remaining details into supporting disclosure.
- Keep Timing, Journal, Reflection, check-in, and evidence below the reading with clearer visual hierarchy.
- Reorder Pattern so “What most shapes you” leads, followed by the rising-sign lens and chapters.
- Move the chart snapshot, houses, placements, connections, and calculation details into a secondary “Your chart” area.
- Reduce desktop density and make mobile chapter discovery substantially faster.
- Preserve the existing warm-paper palette, serif reading voice, monospace evidence language, and nearly square geometry.

## Verification
- Check Today and Pattern at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Confirm chapter opening, navigation, check-in controls, links, disclosures, and technical content still work.
- Confirm the app builds without errors.
