## Case 1 — Communication

This supports a possible work-message scene and renewed attention to expression, not a fact about the reader's current messages or motives.

### Proposed editorial extension

Proposed source text for a writing frame tied specifically to `conjunction:mercury:mercury`:

> When revising a message, a person may make the wording more agreeable while leaving the point less visible. In that kind of draft, the writer may feel that their own position is missing from the message. This Mercury return can be used as a lens for recognizing the difference between improving a sentence and editing out what one means. If that tension is familiar, writing the central point in one plain sentence offers a way to hear it before choosing the final wording. Clarity can preserve warmth; it does not determine how a message will be received or require the message to be sent.

**Rationale:** The repeated Mercury contact concerns a return to articulation and fresh phrasing. The extension explores a specific obstacle within that activity—editing away the point—and the possible experience of no longer hearing one's own position in the resulting draft. That experience is conditional on the described editing, not caused by the contact. It does not infer people-pleasing, a personality trait, conflict, or an actual work event.

**Coverage:** P1's possible draft and its competing aims: I1/D1 plus proposed motive. P2's contact: C1/I1; the distinction between polish and meaning: proposed extension. P3's optional sentence exercise and desired expression: proposed extension. Headline and reflection: the same articulation theme. No unsupported factual claims intended.
