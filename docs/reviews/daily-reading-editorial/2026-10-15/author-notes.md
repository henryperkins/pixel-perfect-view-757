# Source and policy notes

These notes describe the current revision after [human review 1](human-review-1.md). Earlier flat source paragraphs remain in the [human-reviewed archive](human-reviewed-1/README.md). They are superseded as writing proposals, not silently relabeled as approved sources.

## Proposed separation of source material

The exact new source text is in [source-proposals.json](source-proposals.json). This is a documentation proposal, **not the production content schema or a changed prompt**. Each case has four distinct blocks:

| Block | Contents | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Case ID, selected source keys, proposed target | Traceability and later source selection. |
| `writingMaterial` | Meaning, scene, imagery, direction, reflection perspective | The prose material a writer may develop. |
| `constraints` | Personal/causal/outcome limits and preserved conditions | Controls for generation and review; never narrative material to paraphrase. |
| `evidence` | Existing support, explicitly new ideas, contact-specific rationale | Independent source review. |

Meaning and limits must remain separate in a later implementation too; merely moving them in this document does not prevent leakage in a real prompt. Conditions on a meaning still apply even when their wording is not copied into the prose. General observations and comparisons can avoid unprovided personal claims without repeated hedge stacks.

All scenarios remain **2026-10-11 UTC**, with the original inputs, saved bundle, selected contacts, and warm/standard override unchanged. The Jupiter fixture's original technical presentation remains archived. New imagery is not geometry, a measured planetary effect, or already-approved interpretation.
