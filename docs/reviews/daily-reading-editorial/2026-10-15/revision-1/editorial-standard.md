# Pattern daily reading — proposed editorial standard

**Reader promise:** A recognizable moment, a clearer sense of why it matters, and a choice worth considering. This is a proposed standard for human review; it has not changed the application or its writing policy.

## The reading

1. **Recognize something lived.** Start with one possible situation a person can picture. Use a natural condition when the situation is unknown. No presumed events, feelings, habits, or knowledge of someone else's thoughts.
2. **Give it meaning.** Name the tension beneath the situation: belonging and preference, enthusiasm and capacity, expression and self-editing. Explain what is at stake without diagnosing the reader. Include pleasure, interest, and possibility across the set.
3. **Connect the astrology.** Weave in the selected contact and its supported meaning. Geometry does not establish a personal event or an emotion. New interpretive meaning needs an explicit source proposal outside the reading. A day without a selected contact gets no invented cosmic explanation.
4. **Offer direction.** One optional, modest response connected to that tension. It should be usable today and leave room to decline. No promises, pressure, prescriptions, or claims that a transit makes a choice necessary.
5. **Leave an earned reflection.** One separate question, at most 220 characters, that deepens this reading's insight. It must stand on its own and need no answer to make the reading useful.

These are composition aims, not five labeled sections or a mandatory order. Vary the rhythm. Keep the source bookkeeping outside the reading.

## Shape and voice

- A human theme headline, normally 3–9 words.
- Usually 130–200 body words in 2–4 short paragraphs; a quiet reading usually 60–110 words. Do not pad to reach a number.
- One central tension, at most two closely related possible scenes, and one useful response. A reflection is an invitation, not another assignment.
- Warm, direct, ordinary language. Enough emotional depth to matter without a motivational speech, therapy language, hedge stacks, or a repeated reassurance formula.
- Original Pattern prose. The selected Refinery29 [weekly example](https://www.refinery29.com/en-au/weekly-horoscopes-march-29-to-april-4-meaning-effects) connects personal tensions with choices; the [monthly example](https://www.refinery29.com/en-au/march-monthly-horoscope-2026-meanings-effects) develops emotional themes over time. These are craft references, not copy or chart sources.
- Quiet means no contact selected by this reading. It does not mean life is calm or that nothing matters. An ordinary invitation can be useful without astrological attribution.

## Review bar

Score each dimension 1–5: **recognition** (can I locate this in life?), **meaning** (does it help me understand something?), **useful direction** (is there a proportionate choice?), **distinctness** (does this offer its own insight?), and **voice/readability** (does it sound natural and respectful?). A 1 misses the aim; 3 is partially useful but generic or uneven; 4 is clear and worthwhile; 5 is unusually precise and memorable.

Each target needs at least 4 in every dimension plus a separate source review with no unresolved unsupported factual, personal, predictive, or causal claims. Judge quiet-day distinctness by honest, useful latitude. Identify interchangeable readings, overbearing advice, hollow reassurance, or a set dominated by productivity.

Internal scores are editorial judgments, not evidence of member outcomes. Optional suggestions and richer emotional language still require alignment with Pattern's current no-instruction policy.
