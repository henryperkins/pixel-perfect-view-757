## Case 1 — Communication

Scenario: 2026-10-11 UTC. Fixture: `writing-reply-scene`.

### Before — recomputed template baseline using saved content

#### mercury conjunction natal mercury

A cycle of thought and communication resets with fresh curiosity. Mercury's conjunction with natal Mercury sharpens interest in new phrasing, questions, or ideas, bringing mental focus back to how you articulate what you mean.

Pace, scope, and the part of a task that feels like yours are separate pieces of working on something. An unfinished list or a draft with competing aims can be a place to notice friction between those pieces.

Timing: exact yesterday. This reading falls in the middle part of the calculated window.

**Reflection:** What topic or question is currently holding your attention?

### After — editorial target draft, revision 3

#### When the point gets edited out

If you've rewritten a work message until every sentence sounds agreeable, the point you wanted to make may have become harder to find. Perhaps the question has become a suggestion, or an important reservation is buried under explanations. In that kind of draft, it can feel as though your own position has gone missing.

Making a sentence kinder and making its meaning smaller are different things. Writing the central point once, in the words you would use out loud, can help you hear the difference. That sentence can sit beside the reply while you work on its wording.

Mercury meeting your natal Mercury is like a messenger rereading the original note before handing it over. The wording is open to revision; the point can remain legible.

**Reflection:** When is directness the kinder choice?

## Proposed separation of source material

The exact new source text is in [source-proposals.json](source-proposals.json). This is a documentation proposal, **not the production content schema or a changed prompt**. Each case has four distinct blocks:

| Block | Contents | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Case ID, selected source keys, proposed target | Traceability and later source selection. |
| `writingMaterial` | Meaning, scene, imagery, direction, reflection perspective | The prose material a writer may develop. |
| `constraints` | Personal/causal/outcome limits and preserved conditions | Controls for generation and review; never narrative material to paraphrase. |
| `evidence` | Existing support, explicitly new ideas, contact-specific rationale | Independent source review. |

Meaning and limits must remain separate in a later implementation too; merely moving them in this document does not prevent leakage in a real prompt. Conditions on a meaning still apply even when their wording is not copied into the prose. General observations and comparisons can avoid unprovided personal claims without repeated hedge stacks.

All scenarios remain **2026-10-11 UTC**, with the original inputs, saved bundle, selected contacts, and warm/standard override unchanged. The Jupiter fixture's original technical presentation remains archived. New imagery is not geometry, a measured planetary effect, or already-approved interpretation.
