# Pattern daily reading — observational voice

**Reader promise:** Notice one recognizable thing, offer a distinction that changes how it looks, and leave a useful possibility. The user selected case 5's tone in [human review 1](human-review-1.md). This revised standard guides editorial drafts; it does not change runtime policy.

## Teach the voice through examples

| Aim | Writing to build on | Habit to remove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observe | “a pause or a shift in tone can stay with you longer than the words” | Announcing “Your day may still be full of feeling or activity.” |
| Distinguish | Making a sentence kinder and making its meaning smaller. | Reassuring that a difference does not mean something is wrong. |
| Permit | “‘I felt unsettled, and I'm not sure why’ can be an honest place to leave it.” | Consoling that enjoying a few minutes “can be enough.” |
| Turn the headline | “Before a feeling becomes a verdict” | A mood label or a general promise of space. |

Warmth comes from accurate attention. The reading has an observer's voice. No motivational speech, therapeutic diagnosis, hollow reassurance, or polite explanation of its own rules.

## Compose a reading

- Start with a gesture, a sound, a visible detail, or a general observation. A condition can locate an unknown situation; it is not a compulsory opening. Never assert an unprovided personal event or another person's thoughts.
- Develop one human distinction. Use at most two closely related possible scenes. Include desire, interest, connection, and pleasure alongside difficulty.
- Use the selected astrology's imagery where it adds meaning. A comparison can be vivid without claiming a planet caused a feeling or event. New symbolism must be identified and reviewed as proposed source material. Do not reduce the contact to a keyword list or explain that it is “a symbolic lens.”
- Direction can mean noticing, waiting, asking, articulating, letting something stand, or enjoying. It may be implicit in the distinction; it need not be a final action paragraph. Prefer one proportionate possibility over a task or speech assignment in every reading.
- Add one separate reflection of at most 220 characters. Change the angle: another person's perspective, the value beneath a habit, or a wider implication. Repeating the suggested action as a question does not deepen the reading.

## Vary the set

Use a human headline, normally 3–9 words. Bodies usually run 130–200 words in 2–4 paragraphs, or 60–110 for a quiet day; record shorter complete readings instead of padding them. Vary paragraph count, where astrology enters, and how a reading closes. Check repeated structures, scripts, and words across the whole set, including headlines and reflections. Use counts as evidence, not quotas that create another template.

With no selected contact, choose an ordinary observation with its own distinction. Leave the day's emotional character undescribed. The reading need not announce how selection works. Direction can take any of the forms above; enjoyment is one option. Different quiet-day observations must offer different insights, with no invented chart meaning, urgency, or promised calm.

## Separate material from controls

Proposed sources separate **writing material** (meaning, imagery, possible scene, directions) from **constraints** and **provenance/evidence**. Only writing material is prose to develop. Constraints govern the writer and review; their wording must not become the reading. Keep actual conditions and distinctions intact when changing expression. This is a documented proposal, not an implemented prompt or content schema.

Original Pattern prose remains the aim. ## Review

Score recognition, meaning, useful direction, distinctness, and voice/readability from 1–5: 1 misses; 3 is uneven; 4 is worthwhile; 5 is unusually precise. An internal target is at least 4 in each dimension plus no unresolved source-grounding defects. Check whether each reading has value without its final action or reflection. Quiet-day distinctness comes from observation, not a fabricated signal.

AI review cannot override the user's verdict or establish member benefit. Optional guidance, imagery, and source proposals still need explicit alignment before runtime use. Human review is recorded against exact revisions; approval of a tone does not approve unseen drafts.
