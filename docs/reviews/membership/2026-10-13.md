## Delivered behavior

Plans offers $8/month and $72/year with one seven-day trial per account. Stripe Checkout collects the card; the confirmation page refreshes authoritative subscription state. Signed, deduplicated webhooks record renewal and payment state. Paid access only extends after a paid invoice, and access expires at the stored trial/paid-period boundary. Canceling renewal retains access through that boundary. Account deletion immediately cancels subscriptions and expires open checkouts before erasing the account, and fails closed if billing cancellation fails.

The server returns only chapter openings to free/expired accounts, including through MCP. The browser refreshes entitlement-dependent chapter queries when membership changes or expires. Full saved text stays encrypted in the records plane.

Account confirmation, password reset, email change and password change are implemented. Recovery/change tokens are hashed, expiring, bound to the original account state and consumed once. Current-password checks protect email/password changes; successful credential changes revoke existing sessions. Email links use the canonical app origin and fragments, and need explicit submission so email scanners cannot consume them. Sending rejects unsuccessful provider responses and records safe delivery metadata. Account owners can inspect delivery status from Context & privacy.

Connected assistants, assistant sign-in, the consent page and the provider's consent-route alias are active. OAuth uses PKCE; Pattern identity linking and per-client content permissions are checked before and after reads. Journal is off by default. Tools read saved content only, and revocation stops further reads with an existing token.

The operator authorized Gemini Enterprise for member writing after the Lovable gateway reported insufficient credit. New member writing chooses Gemini Enterprise when its key is configured, otherwise Lovable. `PATTERN_MEMBER_LANGUAGE_PROVIDER` can explicitly choose either. Selection occurs before the request; failures never retry through another provider. Daily and natal output record the selected provider's model identity. Existing saved writing retains its original identity. Admin evaluation selection remains independent, and Privacy names both serving paths.
