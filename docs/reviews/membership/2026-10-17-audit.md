# Purchase and account audit — 2026-10-17

## Current business and technical flow

- Catalog: Pattern Plus, USD 8/month (`plus_monthly`) or USD 72/year (`plus_yearly`), one seven-day card-required trial per Pattern account. Both prices were confirmed through the connected Stripe sandbox on October 17.
- Authentication: Pattern owns password accounts and hashed opaque HttpOnly-cookie sessions in D1. Assistant authentication is a separate hosted identity, explicitly linked to Pattern; email matching does not grant access. D1 operations pass through the separately deployed signed `pattern-workflows` Worker.
- Purchase: authenticated `startCheckout` → billing lease → customer mapping/email synchronization → duplicate-subscription and trial checks → price verification → idempotent Stripe Checkout creation → hosted redirect. The current implementation obtains a raw test Stripe key from the Worker and calls Stripe directly, despite the project having a connected Stripe gateway.
- Confirmation: the member-only return page verifies Checkout customer and account ownership, retrieves current Stripe subscription state, then invalidates membership/chapter queries. Pending payments do not grant access.
- Renewal: signature-checked webhooks deduplicate events and retrieve current subscriptions. D1 stores trial end, paid end, current period, status and cancellation. Paid end currently advances from a paid latest invoice using the subscription item's current period end, rather than validated paid invoice line periods.
- Entitlement: server-side natal chapter and delegated assistant reads return chapter openings to free/expired members. Chart, daily reading, journal and openings are free. Renewing subscriptions receive a 24-hour grace extension unless `past_due` or cancellation is scheduled. Disabling memberships currently grants full access to everyone.
- Account updates: email/password changes require current password; recovery and changes use hashed, expiring, one-use tokens. Password reset/change and email change revoke Pattern sessions. Email change attempts to synchronize Stripe email after completion. Assistant grants survive password recovery.
- Export/reset/delete: export includes saved member data and membership state, not full Stripe or hosted-assistant records. Personalization reset is not deletion. Deletion stops open checkouts and subscriptions before erasing D1 data and local assistant access; partial failure can leave an existing account with canceled billing and a deletion flag.

## Confirmed gaps

### Payment integration and accounting

1. Direct Stripe SDK transport and Worker credential export bypass the required shared gateway client.
2. Checkout redirects off-site instead of using `ui_mode: "embedded_page"` and the Stripe embedded components.
3. Customer metadata uses `pattern_user_id`, not the shared `userId` discovery convention; customer resolution depends only on the local mapping.
4. Test/live customers, memberships, locks, events and checkout attempts lack an environment namespace. Only test keys/events/session IDs are supported; UI notices are hardcoded as test mode.
5. Sales availability and authorization are conflated: disabling sales unlocks all chapters.
6. Paid coverage comes from current subscription period rather than the actual paid invoice service periods. Adjustments/prorations and historical paid invoices are not accounted for correctly.
7. Unsupported prices, multiple items and quantities silently retain stale entitlement rows. Historical prices have no explicit mapping/grandfathering policy.
8. No explicit monthly/yearly change or resume-renewal flow; portal capabilities are not validated against the supported catalog.
9. No refund/dispute entitlement policy or handlers, durable billing event queue, dead-letter reporting, or scheduled reconciliation.
10. Fixed three-minute billing leases have no renewal or fencing.
11. No tax codes or tax handling; existing prices have unspecified tax behavior. US seller country was confirmed for the connected sandbox, but product eligibility for full managed compliance still needs verification.
12. Pending confirmation needs a typed pending state, bounded automatic refresh, and a recovery outcome. Provider errors can become generic server errors.
13. Catalog amounts are duplicated in presentation code. Portal currently replaces the app tab instead of opening a separate tab.

### Account security and recovery

1. No application signup/login attempt controls; nonexistent-user login omits equivalent password derivation; signup reveals registered addresses. External WAF protection is unverified.
2. Email confirmation does not gate purchases or assistant linking.
3. Recovery does not revoke assistant grants; no independent sign-out-all control or device/session management.
4. Ordinary verification consumes unrelated reset/change tokens. A failed resend invalidates the preceding usable link.
5. Email-change delivery diagnostics check the old address and presentation ignores recorded local attempts.
6. No old-address email-change alert or password-change notification in the inspected flows.
7. Deletion cancellation can precede a failing final account-state check or deletion transaction; recovery is not an explicit resumable operation.
8. Unfinished recovery loses its token after refreshing; confirmation copy ignores whether sign-in is required.
9. Separate hosted-assistant recovery/account management and export/deletion boundaries need clearer member-facing handling.

### Operational verification gaps

- Live onboarding: sandbox claim complete; Stripe go-live form in progress; live app installation, key provisioning and readiness check not started. No real payments can be accepted yet.
- Email: the previous release recorded a bounced/suppressed recipient. Successful current confirmation/reset inbox delivery, sender status, hosted redirect settings and production configuration have not been requalified.
- Worker migrations/deployment parity, backup/restore, configuration replay protections and credential-export removal require coordinated external Worker work, not just application publication.
- Current browser purchase, recovery, cancellation and deletion journeys have not yet been exercised in this audit.

## Evidence and tests

Primary files: `src/lib/billing.server.ts`, `billing-config.server.ts`, `billing-policy.ts`, `billing.functions.ts`, `auth.server.ts`, `account-security.server.ts`, `account-data.server.ts`; membership settings/plans/confirmation and account-link pages; D1 membership migration; `edge/pattern-workflows/worker.mjs`.

Executed this audit: `node --test` against membership, account-security, account-data, assistant-access, assistant-migration and assistant-readers suites: **54 passed, zero failed**. These tests validate the current direct/hosted Stripe architecture, not embedded gateway checkout. Independent billing/Worker audit also reported 38 targeted passes. No manual build/typecheck was run; no application code was changed. Available preview diagnostic logs contained no reported errors.

## Decisions before the repair plan

- Monthly/yearly switching timing and proration; cancellation/resumption and refund/dispute access policy.
- Failed-payment grace, whether sandbox purchases unlock published content, and trial scope after deletion/recreation.
- Verification gate and assistant revocation during account recovery.
- Tax-inclusive versus tax-exclusive listed prices; historical price treatment.

## Preview acceptance checklist for the eventual implementation

1. Use an isolated test account and a working inbox; complete confirmation. Never modify a real member's birth profile or billing.
2. Confirm the test-mode notice, free openings and in-page monthly/yearly checkout.
3. Use `4242 4242 4242 4242`, a future expiry and any three-digit CVC. Verify trial, membership readback and full chapter access through the UI after reload.
4. Exercise `4000 0000 0000 0002` (decline) and `4000 0025 0000 3155` (authentication). With a free trial, an immediate successful payment is not implied; use an authorized sandbox test clock/shortened test trial to verify real invoice settlement.
5. Verify duplicate checkout protection, closing/reopening checkout, return processing, refresh and delayed settlement without premature access.
6. Test plan changes, scheduled cancellation, resume, exact access expiry and failed renewal with controlled sandbox time; verify authoritative payment and stored member state agree.
7. Confirm password reset/change, email change/new sign-in, token replay rejection, session revocation and the chosen assistant-recovery policy using actual received emails.
8. Delete only a disposable test account; verify subscriptions stop and data removal completes, including recovery from simulated partial failure in regression tests.
9. Test refunds/disputes, missing webhook reconciliation and unsupported prices in isolated sandbox fixtures; never manufacture live payments.
10. Live acceptance requires completed Stripe onboarding and user-approved publication; preview success is not live readiness.
