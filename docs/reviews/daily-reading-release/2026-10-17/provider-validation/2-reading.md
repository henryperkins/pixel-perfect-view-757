# A different plan, the same company

Case: venus-opposition-venus. Result: template; validator: editorial_claim.

Wanting to see someone and wanting a different kind of evening can both be true. An invitation to go dancing can leave a reply hovering at 'I'd love to' when a walk is what appeals. The tender part can be the fear that a different preference will sound like less affection.

Venus opposite your natal Venus is an invitation with two versions of the evening written on it. One question to ask is which part of the plan matters most to each of you. The wish to meet can stay in the conversation while the plan changes.

Reflection: What makes a counterproposal feel like an invitation to you?
