# Before a feeling becomes a verdict

Case: fallback-hard-neptune-mercury. Result: model; validator: accepted.

A pause or a shift in tone can linger long after a conversation ends. In returning to a remembered exchange, one can often catch an explanation being quietly added to the memory. An immediate impression and the story formed around it are distinct things, and a feeling can deserve attention even while another person's intentions remain unknown.

Where Neptune blurs, Mercury names; a square between transiting Neptune and natal Mercury pictures that gap between an impression and the story that gathers to explain it. That impression can simply remain an impression for a while. Acknowledging that you felt unsettled, without immediately knowing why, can be an honest place to leave it.
