# What 'just' leaves out

Case: jupiter-trine-sun-technical. Result: model; validator: accepted.

Speaking with animation about an interest only to call it "just a hobby" puts a qualification immediately after the enthusiasm. Enjoyment can be a reason for an interest to belong in one's self-description before it ever becomes an achievement.

Like transiting Jupiter forming a trine to the natal Sun, the shift is like a wider frame placed around a familiar self-portrait, allowing an existing interest to be included without requiring someone to become a different person. Dropping "just" once can leave the interest stated without a ranking of how accomplished or important it is.

Reflection: What draws you to someone else's interests before you know how skilled they are?
