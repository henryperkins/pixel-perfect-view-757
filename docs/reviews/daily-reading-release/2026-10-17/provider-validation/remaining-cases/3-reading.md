# When enthusiasm becomes a promise

Case: synthesis-tension. Result: model; validator: accepted.

When an idea matters, a smaller commitment can seem to understate enthusiasm. Finding that 'I can do this much' sounds smaller than the interest behind it, promising more may become a way of trying to show how much one cares.

Consider transiting Jupiter's trine to natal Mars as a lengthening stride, with transiting Saturn's square to natal Sun as the pack that comes along; a faster pace does not make the pack lighter. While expansion can increase the structure needed, enthusiasm and the scope of a promise can be expressed separately.

Before adding a piece of work, one can distinguish wanting that work from wanting the promise to express enthusiasm. The size of a commitment need not carry all the expression of interest.

Reflection: What would taking an idea seriously look like at a scale you can manage?
