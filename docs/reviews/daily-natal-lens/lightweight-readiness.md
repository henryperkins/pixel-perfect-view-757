current writing-readiness gate. It replaces exhaustive paired model inventories,
two full reviewer passes and catalogue-wide evidence as prerequisites for this
writing decision. Existing full-benchmark results remain historical evidence;
they are not relabeled as passes. Both candidates stay inactive pending the
appropriate readiness check and a separate activation request.

This set covers natal writing only. It does not qualify transit-candidate chapter
copy; that candidate needs its own small representative review before activation.

The purpose is to establish that a few representative readings offer useful,
readable distinctions, stay within their supplied facts, and fall back safely.
The app's existing functionality does not depend on completing a research-scale
benchmark. No source, provider, publication or activation behavior changes with
this documentation update.

## One primary review

One fresh reviewer sees the exact prose, supplied facts and tone/depth/quiet
limits. Use opaque sample IDs and uniformly masked paragraph citation arrays;
retain the originals and source hashes separately. Deduplicate only identical
complete contexts. The collection must say **targeted review of partial-run
evidence**, retain links to the missing/unattempted inventory and make no full
benchmark claim. Do not weaken the existing complete-report tools to prepare it.

Use the existing specificity, grounding and coherence standards: each must be
at least **4/5**, as must lens integration or synthesis coherence when supplied.
Keep the duty/freedom practical distinction. Every selected raw draft and template
must meet the applicable checks, with no unresolved substantive finding. The
quiet item must remain safe; numerical prose scores are inapplicable there.
Voice and repetition observations should focus on useful wording changes, rather
than becoming extra measurement projects.

A second opinion is needed only for a particular disputed or uncertain finding;
it does not repeat the whole collection. Preserve both judgments, resolve the
specific passage against its facts, and never average away a factual defect or
quietly replace a low score. If the prose needs a fix, make that fix and review
only affected cases plus relevant deterministic regressions.

Record one short results table and exact quotes for material defects. Packet/source
hashes and one saved original review are sufficient provenance for this set.
Separate AI assessment from human approval; a human-review program is not a
prerequisite for this writing gate.
