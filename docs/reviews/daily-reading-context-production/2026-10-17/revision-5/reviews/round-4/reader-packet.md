# Reader packet

Read only the body prose below. Each body is an independent response to one controlled shared-contact scenario, not a consecutive-day sequence. Judge recognition, meaning, direction, distinctness and voice on 1–5, where 3 is below the bar, 4 is solid and acceptable, and 5 is unusually strong. Every dimension must reach 4 for a body to pass; there is no averaging.

The requested voice notices a concrete detail, names a useful distinction and gives optional, proportionate direction; warmth comes from accuracy, not counseling or reassurance. Flag formal language, repeated meaning (including the same point made again in different words), a dictated script, appended personality, or chronology without practical meaning. Shared imagery across bodies is not penalized; repetition and missing development within a body are. Do not score source accuracy: you have no sources.
