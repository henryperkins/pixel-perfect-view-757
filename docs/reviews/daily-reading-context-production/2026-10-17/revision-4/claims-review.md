# Observed claim boundaries, revision 4

This revision adds three narrow checks to the context candidate validator. It does not change the established member or synthetic route when the context candidate is absent. No provider request, remote mutation or publication was performed for this work.

The previous natal-only capture was already rejected for “can easily attach itself.” That first rejection masked two further unsupported additions: “could simply be left out” adds ease to an optional removal, and the supplied notes/tab image is made to “blur where your attention belongs.” Removing the first detected adverb previously allowed both remaining additions through automatic validation. The synthesis capture was rejected earlier by the separate safety guard; direct claim validation also previously missed its unsupported initiative-causes-appeal relationship.

The new checks bind the relevant local subject and predicate to the reviewed `0.9.0` source key:

- `I1`, `square:saturn:sun`: an extra check or another version cannot acquire ease through a modal + “simply” + removal predicate.
- `N1`, `nl-sun-03`: notes or a tab do not establish an effect that blurs where attention belongs.
- `I2`, `trine:jupiter:mars`: initiative's reciprocal reinforcement with enthusiasm does not establish that initiative makes or causes another piece to look, seem or feel appealing.

Punctuation and coordinating clauses bound each check. Conditions and modals do not supply an absent property or causal relationship. The checks do not ban “simply,” notes, tabs, initiative or appeal as isolated words. Tests retain the supported optional removal, separate note, initiative/enthusiasm reinforcement and appealing-piece illustration, including ordinary paraphrases. Different source keys and releases are outside these observed-source checks.

The regression tests first failed in four expected places against unchanged production code. After the changes, the three claim test files passed **34 tests, 0 failures**. The integration regressions preserve rejected raw paragraphs losslessly, use the full fallback and make one selected-provider attempt without retry. They also verify that established non-context member and synthetic behavior is unchanged and that the original five benchmarks remain structurally accepted. Scoped ESLint and `git diff --check` passed. Logs are in the private revision-4 evidence directory; the parent integration run supplies final whole-suite verification.

These are deterministic checks for observed predicates. They do not establish semantic grounding, recognize every paraphrase, prove that each selected meaning survives, or assess voice. In particular, the natal capture's omitted effort meaning and the synthesis capture's incomplete I1 meaning remain independent source-review concerns. A version with the three additions removed can pass this bounded validator while still failing source review. The new pilot therefore still requires independent review of every exact raw body before any mode qualifies for controlled activation.
