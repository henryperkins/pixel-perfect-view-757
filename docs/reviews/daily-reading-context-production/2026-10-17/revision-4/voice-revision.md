# Context writer revision 4: voice and meaning

This is an authored prompt and material revision, not generated-quality evidence or approval to activate. Independent source review must assess the changed material before the paid pilot. Captured pilot bodies then need independent reader and source reviews at the existing thresholds.

## What failed

The preceding production pilot's blind reader review found that four bodies fell below the voice bar. Three also repeated or stacked meaning instead of developing it. The source review found unsupported ease in the natal-only body, an invented notes-to-attention effect, an invented initiative-to-appeal relationship in the synthesis body, and incomplete selected meanings. No generated body met all gates.

The actual assembled candidate request was inspected. Its candidate branch does not include the legacy prompt, benchmark bodies, member history or fallback prose. The remaining competition was inside the candidate material: the I1 control summary supplied the same formal expectations/capacity/boundary sentence reproduced across failed bodies, and I1 and P2 both supplied rescheduling-versus-size as their practical distinction. That encouraged an abstract summary followed by the same scheduling observation twice. The required coverage of several sources in the first two paragraphs also made a list of mini-readings an easy response.

## The changes

Prompt, voice, brief and material versions advance to 4. The validator identity also advances to reference-4 for the separately implemented unsupported-predicate checks. The original benchmark bodies, exact source facts, selected source identities, published bundle and runtime fallback bodies are unchanged.

Material uses familiar words and complete, locally qualified meanings. It offers short building blocks rather than complete answer bodies. Source authority remains separate from drafting material. `historicalReviewSupport` explicitly records material-3 field paths and historical evidence; `revisionSupport` and `voice-material-changes.json` identify the newly authored equivalents requiring review.

| I1, with C1 | Possible extra effort, then what fits | A familiar responsibility can need more effort than usual. The extra check needing some of the same hours carries the conditional capacity/limit meaning. The drafting material does not need a second formal explanation of expectations, capacity and boundaries. |

| I2, with C2 | Enthusiasm together with a practical start | Under the well-chosen-aim condition, enthusiasm and practical initiative can support one another and taking up more work can feel easier. Neither initiative creating appeal nor ease guaranteeing results is supplied. |
| S1, with C1/I1/C2/I2 | What a larger task needs | Added scope can need more structure, even when starting feels easy. This connects the two parent meanings through the same potential addition. It does not establish actual excess scope or prescribe expansion. |

The brief includes a `development` map: the central situation is I1, and each selected meaning receives one distinct purpose. This is a coverage aid, not an ordered outline. It contains source IDs and role labels, no sentence sequence, paragraph count or authored body. Unselected source roles are absent. The writer still chooses the opening detail, ordering, rhythm and supported illustrations. The map is intended to prevent the return chapter from repeating the primary scheduling point and the synthesis from becoming a separate lesson.

The system and voice instructions ask the writer to keep one possible situation, make its concrete cost legible, add a different piece of meaning with each selected context, and stop when the supported distinction or choice is clear. They explicitly reject an abstract restatement after a concrete sentence has already made the point. Natural direct address and contractions are permitted. No fixed script, closing permission sentence, paragraph sequence or contact-name order is introduced.

An illustration authorizes no new effect. General prompt instructions state that limit without importing unselected source imagery. The notes/tab warning appears only with selected natal context. The reciprocal initiative condition is inside the I2 card, so contact-only requests do not receive an unselected I2 interpretation.

## Evidence and limits

A new behavioral regression first failed because the brief had no development map. It then passed after the projection was implemented. Across all five real fixture assemblies, it checks that every selected meaning has exactly one distinct role, no unselected source role is projected, source summaries and complete benchmark paragraphs are absent from the planning map, and projected edits cannot mutate subsequent requests.

The focused voice/material/writer/member suite passes 26 tests. It exercises the real intercepted provider boundary, all five source graphs, exact chronology, source drift, missing context, claim qualifiers, provider failure without failover, and immutable authored fallbacks. Interception makes no paid provider request. The red and green logs are retained in the private revision-4 evidence directory.

These checks establish the request contract and preserved source identities. They cannot establish that a provider will write well. That verdict belongs to the new five-body pilot and independent reviews; no threshold has changed.
