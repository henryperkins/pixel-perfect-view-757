Two authored body revisions now clear the independent AI reader threshold of 4/5 in recognition, meaning, direction, distinctness and voice. Both pass separate source review. Read the [edited readings](revised-readings.md), [reader review](reader-review.md) and [source review](source-review.md). ## Applied to the inactive writer

Version 3 moves abstract source summaries out of draftable material into `controls.sourceMeanings`. Scene details, practical distinctions and optional directions remain drafting material. Source conditions, identity bindings and complete parent facts remain intact. The I1/N1 building blocks now reflect the reviewed edits; the other three cards are unchanged. Generic editing instructions favor a visible detail, one consequence and a proportionate ending, with no minimum word target or required script. Full edited bodies, review examples and audit metadata stay outside provider requests. The [material review](material-source-review.md) checks exact bytes and all five projected source graphs.

The candidate-only qualifier guard rejects the observed unsupported `quickly`/`easily` additions and source-modal loss across intervening adverbs. A modal in another clause cannot qualify the definite source claim. An I2 destination phrase alone cannot license ease for an unrelated extra-check claim. Source-supported I2 ease remains accepted. Rejected output is retained losslessly, and the complete original benchmark fallback survives one attempt without repair or provider switching.

These checks are deliberately lexical and bounded. Changed subjects, arbitrary paraphrases, distant dependencies and other semantic strengthening still need source review. Comma-isolated supported ease may conservatively reject. Normal member writing and ordinary synthetic routing are unchanged.
