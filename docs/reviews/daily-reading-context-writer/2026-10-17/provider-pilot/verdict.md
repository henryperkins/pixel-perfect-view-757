**Hold the candidate.** The production experiment completed within its limits, but none of the five generated bodies meets the authored benchmark across all reader dimensions. Four preserve source meaning; the contact-only body introduces an unsupported recent-onset claim. Automatic acceptance was 5/5 and did not detect these editorial or semantic failures.

Four bodies open with essentially the same source sentence: “An established role can feel demanding when its obligations require more deliberate effort than usual.” The fifth paraphrases it. Boundaries, curiosity and adjustment are explained, but rarely given the visible consequence that made the benchmarks useful. The natal body puts project exploration beside pressure with “At the same time.” The transit body reproduces chronology and the chapter's general adjustment idea. Combined context stacks those meanings instead of changing how one situation can be understood. The synthesis body does connect scope to the structure needed to carry it, earning 4 for Meaning, but its delivery remains abstract and crowded.

The contact-only body says expectations “have begun to outpace your available capacity.” Neither the source interpretation nor calculated geometry establishes that recent change in the reader's life. Its following “that strain” carries the possibility forward as established. Complete citations and automatic acceptance did not establish semantic support. The other four preserve the supplied conditions, source parents and chronology; combined context adds a minor unsupported “especially” emphasis.

The repeated contact and scene were deliberate comparison controls. This is evidence of weak execution within that comparison, not a consecutive-day study. Other contacts, settings, natal lenses, transit chapters, tone/depth combinations and member outcomes remain untested.

## Next candidate

The saved provider brief supplies full legacy source paragraphs as its material meanings, while the voice rules allow concrete details and optional direction only from those meanings. The authored benchmark body is a fallback, not a writing example in the request. That input design is consistent with the conservative copying observed here; this is a diagnosis from the code and captures, not a proven causal explanation.

The next proposed change is to supply reviewed, source-bound **scene detail, practical distinction and optional direction** as writing material for the primary interpretation and selected context, then compose one situation in which the natal and chapter distinctions have a consequence. Preserve interpretive conditions and test that conditional scope survives across sentences. That proposal needs source review and offline verification before another separately authorized bounded provider pilot. The current campaign is complete and the context-aware member writer remains inactive.
