## Set assessment and limits

The recurring gap is specificity with consequence. The benchmarks use an observable detail to make a distinction useful: leaving an idea in notes, recognizing when an addition changes the agreement, or defining where work stops. R1–R5 tend to state “capacity,” “boundaries,” “curiosity” and “adjustment” without giving those ideas a visible edge. R3's scope/structure relationship is the main exception in meaning, although its delivery is too abstract and dense.

Chronology in R1 and R5 mostly remains appended; R3 packs it into an already crowded paragraph. The conditional language about an earlier method avoids declaring that a prior life event occurred, but that restraint alone does not make the return practically meaningful. This is an observation about the prose, not verification of the calculated facts.

The repeated contact and setting are deliberate controlled conditions and were not treated as a consecutive-day defect. Even within that design, four readings begin with essentially the same “An established role can feel demanding” sentence, and R2 uses a close paraphrase. This exposes a narrow structural range. Broader contact, setting and consecutive-day coverage remains unestablished.

There is no overt policy/system lecturing, dictated script, or planet-keyword list. Those absences do not offset the missing observable detail, repeated meaning and formal abstraction. The set does not meet the approved authored standard on reader quality; source review and any provider or production qualification remain separate.
