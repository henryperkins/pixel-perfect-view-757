## What each reading contributes

**Contact only.** The smaller requests and the extra check are observable examples of a responsibility exceeding its available capacity. The distinction between rearranging the calendar and changing the boundary follows the selected Saturn–Sun meaning. The closing move is small and optional. “Boundary,” “edge” and the task's size recur a little heavily, which keeps the voice at 4.

**Natal only.** Notes and an open tab turn the selected curiosity lens into a useful distinction: exploring several ideas and accepting several commitments can be separate stages. The natal paragraph changes the practical meaning of the initial situation; it does not become a personality account. Leaving one idea in the notes is permission supported by that distinction. The reflection extends the reader's discernment rather than asking them to repeat an exact suggested sentence.

**Transit only.** The exact calculated chronology is followed immediately by a conditional adjustment to the same task and boundary. The draft does not treat the September contact as proof that this personal problem occurred then. The final stopping point gives the chapter's smaller adjustment a concrete form. The chronology is necessarily more documentary than the surrounding prose, but its meaning is integrated rather than appended as a separate forecast.

**Natal and transit.** This is the strongest demonstration of the candidate's purpose. Curiosity helps explain why several possibilities can remain open, while the return chapter invites a smaller change to an existing method if it no longer fits. The ending combines those ideas into one decision: keep the second idea available and define the commitment that receives this afternoon's hours. The first paragraph is dense, but each context changes how the same scene is considered.

**Synthesis, natal and transit.** The move from a promising outline to another piece of work makes the low-friction initiative meaning recognizable. It retains the condition that the aim is well chosen. The text distinguishes ease in taking on work from the structure needed to carry its larger scope, preserving the actual synthesis relationship. Natal exploration and the conditional return adjustment remain attached to that distinction. “There may be a distinction to make” could be leaner, but the reading remains useful before its final paragraph and does not reduce the parents to planetary keywords.

## Source and chronology findings

- The primary interpretation remains a possibility about expectations and available capacity. No reading asserts that the reader's responsibilities have actually expanded or that Saturn caused a personal event.
- The natal meaning retains possibility and the practical difference between sampling and choosing. The natal-only and combined cases explicitly retain the source's condition that several projects compete for the same afternoon. The synthesis case draws on the first, general possibility that following several lines of curiosity can help a direction take shape, without making the conditioned stages distinction universal. The final drafts do not claim ownership of a Sun-in-Gemini placement from a fact atom that supplies only the selected lens meaning.
- Every transit case reproduces the supplied sentence exactly once: “This is the second of three calculated contacts; the first exact contact falls in September 2026.” Each uses it in paragraph 2, with the chapter interpretation and full parent citations. No prior personal event, unresolved issue or predicted resolution is inferred from the pass count.
- The transit chapter retains its antecedent: an earlier way of handling the task must no longer fit before its smaller adjustment is offered. The drafts do not turn the chapter into a required change.
- The synthesis case keeps the well-chosen-aim condition, possible low-friction effort and possible need for more structure as separate claims. It does not invent a guaranteed causal sequence between them.
- Notes, an outline, an open tab, an extra version and a stopping point are bounded hypothetical illustrations of the supplied project, effort, curiosity and boundary meanings. They introduce no personal history, outside intentions or unsupported outcome.
- No added frequency, necessity, exclusion or causal claim remains. The earlier stride/pack imagery, which belonged to different authored material, has been removed rather than borrowed into this source set.

## Set-level limits

The repeated contact and afternoon/project situation are deliberate controls in the first four variants. They make the effect of adding natal and transit context visible and should not be scored as consecutive-day repetition. The synthesis case adds only the original supporting Jupiter–Mars contact and its selected tension relationship.

This design does not show day-to-day variety. All five readings use three paragraphs, the same broad setting and one chapter/lens combination; they cannot qualify production coverage of quiet days, other contacts, other natal lenses, other transit chapters, tone/depth settings or consecutive days. The candidate writer must still earn its own voice assessment from actual outputs. Reference validation and mocked-provider tests cannot establish that assessment.
